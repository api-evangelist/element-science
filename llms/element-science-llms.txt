# Element Science

> Element Science is a San Francisco-based medical device and digital health company. Its flagship product is the Jewel Patch-Wearable Cardioverter Defibrillator (P-WCD), a low-profile, water-resistant wearable that continuously monitors for and treats life-threatening ventricular arrhythmias in patients at temporary elevated risk of sudden cardiac arrest. The Jewel received FDA Premarket Approval in 2025, and CE Mark / UKCA marking in January 2024. Element Science is a portfolio company of GV.
